# Product

## Register

product

## Users

B2B professionals attending the Logiterre Expo — a logistics industry trade show. Two roles:
- **Exposant** (Exhibitor): company representatives staffing booths, scanning visitor badges, managing their team members on-site
- **Visiteur** (Visitor): logistics professionals attending to discover suppliers, partners, and solutions

Context: used in a busy, loud trade show hall. Interactions are brief — 10-30 seconds per scan. Users are standing, often distracted, with one hand occupied. The primary task on any screen is fast and decisive.

## Product Purpose

A QR badge networking tool that eliminates business-card friction at logistics trade shows. Scan someone's badge → instant digital connection saved to your history. Secondary features: browsing the exhibitor directory, managing booth team members, viewing event countdown and networking stats.

Success = a user leaves the show with a complete, searchable record of every meaningful contact made — without manual data entry.

## Brand Personality

Operational precision. Industrial confidence. Logistics urgency.

The brand does not perform "tech" or "startup." It performs the language of the floor: efficiency, reliability, directness. Like the interface on a premium freight management system — not decorative, not cold, but built for people who move things.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with cream/sand backgrounds and rounded cards everywhere
- Consumer networking apps (LinkedIn-blue, friendly gradients, soft shadows)
- Corporate trade show apps that feel like printed brochures digitized
- Startup-coded dark mode with purple/violet accents and hero metrics
- Anything that looks like it belongs in a wellness or hospitality category

## Design Principles

1. **Scannable at a glance** — every screen must yield its primary information in under 2 seconds. Dense but not cluttered.
2. **Amber as signal, not decoration** — the accent color carries urgency and call-to-action weight only. Never used ornamentally.
3. **Typography does the work** — hierarchy through weight and size contrast, not through decoration, dividers, or color noise.
4. **Trust over delight** — the interface communicates competence first, personality second. No flourishes that slow down the task.
5. **Industrial legibility** — designed for bright trade show lighting and tired eyes. High contrast, no low-opacity muted text that disappears in ambient light.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- High ambient light scenario: all text must pass 4.5:1 in both dark and light mode
- One-handed operation: all primary CTAs reachable in thumb zone
- Reduced motion: all Reanimated animations must have a non-animated fallback
- French-language interface throughout
